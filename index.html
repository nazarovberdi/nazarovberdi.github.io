02 — About

Four years ofshipping to production.

I'm Berdi, a frontend engineer working mostly in Vue, React and TypeScript. At MilliGoshant I build the frontend of Turkmenistan Railways' freight tariff system with a small team, on a Go backend.

Its Vue apps calculate freight charges for local and transit shipments and bill the services stations provide. I'm the main developer of the station-fees app, from printed bills to the overcharge and undercharge corrections that follow them, and I built most of the spreadsheet-style workspace where operators calculate a shipment's charges wagon by wagon.

On my own time I ship products end to end, like Lyne, an EPUB reader on Google Play, and Bileje, a paid invitation app.
